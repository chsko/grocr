# Product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

A parent in a household of two to six people who plans the week's dinners, typically on Sunday
evening on the sofa. Everyone in the family can see the plan and shop from the list, often
one-handed in the store with a trolley or a child in the other hand.

## Product Purpose

Grocr is a family weekly dinner planner. The family keeps its own recipes, picks dinners for the
week, and the ingredients land on a shared grocery list automatically. Success is a family that
plans the week in a few minutes and comes home from the store with everything for those dinners.

## Positioning

Only the family's own recipes: no recipe catalogue, no ads, no content to scroll. The grocery list
fills itself from the menu and syncs live for every member of the family.

## Operating Context

- Invite-only family group; members join with a 6-character code or link.
- Four tabs: Week, Groceries, Recipes, Family. Weeks start on Monday; one grocery list per week;
  from Friday Groceries opens next week's list and offers to carry over unchecked items.
- Grocery items can be ticked, or marked as already at home. Lines are grouped by store section in
  the family's own store order.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- iOS and Android, built with Expo; Convex backend with real-time sync.
- Online only: there is no offline mode.
- Sign in with Apple and Google.
- Norwegian bokmål first, English second, switchable under Family > Language.
- Light and dark appearance, switchable under Family > Appearance.
- Subscription of 5 NOK per month at store launch.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Grocr. Domain grocr.io, support email support@grocr.io.
- Logo: the grocery bag (tomato-red bag, ink handle, green sprig, white "G").
- Look: toned-down "A Market day": mostly white, saffron as soft highlights, tomato red for
  actions, Bricolage Grotesque, small food icons on recipes.

## Product Principles

1. Planning the week should take minutes, not an evening.
2. The list is the family's shared truth; everyone sees the same thing at once.
3. The family's own recipes are the whole library; never pad it with outside content.
4. Built for a busy hand in a busy store: big targets, glanceable state.
